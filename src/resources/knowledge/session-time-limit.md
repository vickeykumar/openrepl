title: How long a session lasts
keywords: session, time, timeout, limit, expire, disconnected, reconnect, 60 minutes, minutes, connection, closed
link: /about.html
---
A connection lasts up to 60 minutes. The terminal shows the time left, and one click starts a new session. If the connection drops, use Reconnect in the terminal bar. For a guest, files are removed an hour after the last visit, so download anything you want to keep or sign up to keep it.
