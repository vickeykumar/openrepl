With the pane showing your page and Agent switched on, try:

- "Make a page with a sign-up form and try it."
- "Draw a logo as an SVG, then make the colors warmer."
- "Show how a bubble algorithm works as a small page I can play with."
- "Make a tiny game, play it, and fix the controls."
- "Why is my page blank?" The errors and failed calls are checked.
- "Check my page at a phone width and fix what is too wide."
- "Click every link and say which are broken."
- "Find images without a description and add one."

The page is written, shown, tried and improved, and each version appears in the pane.
