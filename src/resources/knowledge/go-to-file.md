Press Ctrl+Shift+O (Cmd+Shift+O on a Mac) to open a search over the files of your workspace. Type a few letters of a name, for example srv for server.go; the letters only have to come in order. The best matches come first, with the matched letters underlined and the folder beside each name, and your open tabs are listed first. Press Enter to open the file as a tab. The same window is reached from the command menu (Ctrl+Shift+P) by choosing Go to file, or from the search button at the top of the Files panel. Typing > as the first character switches to the commands, and Backspace on an empty box comes back. Hidden files, the ones whose name starts with a dot, are not listed. Right-click an item in the Files panel and choose Copy path (the full location) or Copy relative path (under your workspace) to put it on your clipboard; with several selected, all of them are copied.
