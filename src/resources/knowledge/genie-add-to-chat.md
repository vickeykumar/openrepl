You can point Genie at exactly what it should look at. In the Files panel, right-click an item and choose Add to Genie chat, or drag it onto the chat. In the editor, select code and click the small Add to chat button next to it, or use the right-click menu, or press Ctrl+Shift+L (Cmd+Shift+L on a Mac). The same works for text you select in the terminal. You can also type # and a few letters of a name in the message box and pick from the list. Each addition is a chip above the message box: click a chip to open it, and the cross to remove it. The dashed chip is the current file: click it to stop Genie looking at the editor. Chips go with the next message and are loaded when you press Send. A message takes 8 chips and about 24,000 characters by default: a longer item is cut and its chip turns amber. Binary and hidden items, and folders such as node_modules, are skipped.
