The Preview works like a small browser. Back and Forward follow the page's links, a thin bar shows loading, and the address bar can be typed in (a page of your workspace, or a port such as 5000/api; Ctrl+L goes there). Up to four tabs hold different pages or ports.

Console shows what the page writes and its errors (a red number counts them); Requests lists the calls it makes with status and time. They answer "why is my page blank?". When a program answers with a server error (500) a banner shows the first lines, with "Show in terminal" and "Show page anyway". The status line says Loading, Live, or that your program has stopped.

The ⋯ menu: Auto-update on save, Reload when I press Run, Clear this preview's cookies, Copy address, and Pop out to its own window (it follows reloads). Run keeps the Preview open: the terminal comes up under it as a drawer, its Hide closes the drawer, and the page reloads.

In a shared session each person has their own pane on the owner's workspace and program, and the cookies a previewed app sets are kept for each person separately.
