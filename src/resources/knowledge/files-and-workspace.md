Each session has a workspace where you can create, upload and download files and folders. You can upload files up to 20 MB, keep up to 50 MB in total, and download the whole workspace as a zip. A guest workspace is deleted about an hour after your last visit. If you sign in, your workspace is kept and is there next time. The Files panel shows the space you have left.
