On the practice page the panel has three coach buttons above the box. Hint gives up to three hints per question, each one a little stronger: a direction, then the approach, then the idea in plain words. A hint never contains code. Review my solution says what is right and wrong, with the inputs that break it, without rewriting your code. Complexity states the time and space cost of your solution and why. Each use is one request. The count of hints used is kept in this browser.
