The Activity panel (the list icon before Close in the panel header) has a Context row that lists each part that goes with your next message, with its size and an estimate of tokens. Grey lines say why something is not sent.

A star on a chat keeps it at the top of the chat list and out of Delete all. The search box there looks in titles and in what was said, and the Starred, Chat and Agent buttons narrow it.

Auto, when the site offers it, sits at the top of the model list. It picks a small model for plain questions and a stronger one for changes, attached items and tasks, and says which one answered. Use a stronger one asks again. Picking a model yourself turns Auto off.

Typing a slash at the start of the box lists the commands with a line about each.
