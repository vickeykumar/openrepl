The Preview pane works like a small browser. Type in its address bar and press Enter: a file of your workspace (web/index.html, counted from your home folder), or a port of your programs (5000, 5000/api, localhost:5000, 127.0.0.1:5000/x); it loads in the tab you are in, and Back returns to the page before. Other websites are not loaded here. The + beside the tabs opens an empty tab with suggestions (your pages, the ports that listen, what you opened lately). The line at the bottom says "sandboxed page".

Move the pane: drag its grip (the dots at the left of its bar) onto the editor to cover the editor and keep the terminal beside it, back onto the terminal to cover the terminal again, or out of the browser window to open it in its own window. The "..." menu has "Dock over the editor" and "Pop out to its own window" for the keyboard. The place is remembered in this browser only.
