In the Files panel and on the tabs, an amber name with an M was changed since you opened the page (saved by you, or written by a program), and a green name with a U is new. A dot after a name means edits that are not saved yet. A folder holding such items is tinted too, so you can see it without opening it. There is no commit to compare with, so the marks last until you reload the page or choose Clear change marks, in the right-click menu of the panel or in the command menu.
