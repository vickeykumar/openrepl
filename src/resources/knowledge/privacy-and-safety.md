Code runs in a sandboxed container created for your session and removed afterwards. Do not paste passwords, keys or other secrets into the terminal, the editor or Genie. Sign-in uses Google Firebase. Code links are readable by anyone who has the link. Genie sends your messages, editor code and recent terminal output to the AI service of the model you choose. The Privacy policy and Terms of use pages have the details.
