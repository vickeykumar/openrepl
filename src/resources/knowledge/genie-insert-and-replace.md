Snippets in the answers have Insert and Replace buttons. Insert puts the suggestion at your cursor. Replace offers it as the new content of the whole editor, and is not available on the practice page. Nothing is applied at once: the change appears over the editor as a diff, with removed text in red and added text in green. Accept or Reject each change, or all of them; Enter accepts and Esc rejects. When all are decided the panel closes and a notice offers Undo for a few seconds. Afterwards Ctrl+Z still undoes it. This is how it works for every answer.
