The canvas is a tab of the Preview pane (More menu, "Open the canvas", or ask Genie to draw). Genie draws screens and layouts as boards on an endless surface; each board is a plain web page saved in your workspace in .genie/canvas/<name>/boards. Pan with the hand tool, Space and drag or the wheel; zoom with Ctrl and scroll (10 to 400 percent); F fits all.

Move a board (it snaps to its neighbours), size it by its corner, duplicate, delete or add one. Every change is a version: Undo, Redo, Versions, Compare. Pin a note: keep it, or send it to Genie with the board and the part under the pin. A selected board shows as a "Board" chip in Genie's box; Genie reads your changes only when you send a message.

Click a part of a board (a button, a heading, a card) to change it: a toolbar floats over it, and text can be typed in at once. The toolbar has Fill, Corner, Text, Layout, Size, Lock, Note and More (spacing, border and shadow, opacity, a picture, order, copy and paste style, hide, a shared style). Shift-click lines several parts up; Try it uses a board like a visitor (buttons, scripts, board links); Alt shows distances; Responsive shows a board at phone, tablet or laptop width. A locked part is never changed, by you or by Genie.

Export one board as HTML, all as a zip, or print as a PDF. A link like href="#board:pricing" goes to that board. Boards run in a sandbox with no network. A canvas holds 24 boards. A shared session sees it live. An admin can switch it off.
