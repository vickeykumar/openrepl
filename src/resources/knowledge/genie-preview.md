With the Preview pane open, Genie can see your page. In Chat a chip "the preview" gives it the title, the headings and the errors of the page with each question; click the chip to stop that.

In Agent mode Genie works with the page like a visitor: it reads it, clicks, types in fields, scrolls, chooses from lists, presses keys, looks at the console and the calls that failed, checks the page at a phone width, and runs a short expression in the page to look at a value.

The first time it acts, a card names the part it will use. Sending a form and following a link out of your workspace are asked every time. It never types in password or card fields, never reaches your account, and stays on pages of your workspace. An admin can switch acting off; reading stays.

It works on web pages and programs that serve pages. Markdown, pictures and PDF have no script, so those are looked at as files.
