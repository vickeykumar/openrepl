Keys for the Preview: Ctrl+Alt+V opens or closes the pane (Ctrl+Shift+V is left to paste as text). F6 moves between the editor, the pane's bar and the page. Pressing Esc twice inside the previewed page returns to the editor. Ctrl+L goes to the address bar. Ctrl+` shows the terminal, also from inside the page.

On a phone the Preview is a third tab beside Editor and Terminal; the Terminal tab closes the pane.

The first time the Preview button shows, a short hint explains it once for each browser; "Show me" opens the pane.
