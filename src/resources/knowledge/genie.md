Genie is OpenREPL's AI helper. Open it with the Ask Genie button or Ctrl+K. It reads the code in your editor and the latest output of your terminal, so you can ask why something fails and insert the suggested fix in one click. You choose the model: GPT-6 Luna, which thinks before it answers and has an effort setting, GPT-4o mini, which answers quickly, or Gemma 4 31B through OpenRouter. Signed-in users get more requests. Genie is optional; nothing is sent to an AI service unless you use it. Do not paste passwords or keys into it.
