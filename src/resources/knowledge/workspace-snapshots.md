title: Workspace snapshots and getting files back

When you are signed in, the server copies your workspace files every few minutes after you change something. There is one copy for each day (UTC), renewed by every save of that day, and the last three days are kept. Open your profile page and look at Workspace snapshots: it lists the days with their time and size, and under a day the earlier versions of it, when the file store still has them. Choose Get back and pick one way: put the files in a new folder next to your files, download them as a zip, or replace your whole workspace. A replace removes the files you have now and saves nothing of them, so copy or download what you want to keep first; it asks you to type REPLACE. Guests have no snapshots, and their files are deleted an hour after the last visit.
