title: Workspace snapshots and getting files back
keywords: snapshot, snapshots, backup, restore, get back, lost files, deleted files, old version, workspace, profile, signed in
link: /profile
---
When you are signed in, the server copies your workspace files every few minutes after you change something, and keeps the newest three copies. Open your profile page and look at Workspace snapshots: it lists them with their time and size. Choose Get back and pick one way: put the files in a new folder next to your files, download them as a zip, or replace your whole workspace. A replace removes the files you have now, so it saves a copy of them first and asks you to type REPLACE. Guests have no snapshots, and their files are deleted an hour after the last visit.
