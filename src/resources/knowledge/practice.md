The Practice page generates coding interview questions. Press New question, choose a topic and a level (easy, medium or hard), and AI writes a fresh question with starter code in C, C++, Go and Python. Leave the topic empty for a surprise. You can also pick a random question. Solve it in the editor next to a live REPL and tick it off when it passes. Your progress by level is shown on the page, saved in your browser, and in your account if you are signed in. The Genie panel there has a coach for hints, a solution review and complexity.
