The Practice DSA page opens with 300+ ready-made DSA problems, shown 10, 20, 40 or 100 to a page, by topic and level (arrays, trees, dynamic programming, graphs and more); a question opens with its description and starter code written when you first open it. You can add your own too: press New question, choose a topic and a level (easy, medium or hard), and AI writes a fresh question with starter code in C, C++, Go and Python. Leave the topic empty for a surprise. You can also pick a random question. Solve it in the editor next to a live REPL and tick it off when it passes. Your progress by level is shown on the page, saved in your browser, and in your account if you are signed in. The Genie panel there has a coach for hints, a solution review and complexity.
