Open the usage line at the bottom of the chat panel to see the details. Below the refill information are the tokens used, as the models report them: what was sent to the model (your message, the code and terminal text, the conversation) and what the model wrote back, thinking included. Without an account you see the tokens of this visit. Signed in, you see this month and all time, on any device, and agent tasks are counted step by step. Each answer also shows its own count under it. Only these numbers are kept, never your messages or code. Nothing is limited by tokens: the limit is the number of requests.
