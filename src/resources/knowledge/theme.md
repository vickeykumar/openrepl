OpenREPL has three theme choices: Light, Dark and System. System is the default: the site follows your device's light or dark setting and changes with it, until you pick Light or Dark yourself. Open the theme button in the top bar (the small sun, moon or screen icon) and choose one. In the Light theme the whole page is light, including the top bar, the first section and the footer; in the Dark theme the whole page is dark. The code editor, the terminal and the code samples stay dark in both, and the IDE follows the editor theme you pick in its status bar. Your choice is remembered in this browser. You can also use Ctrl+K and type "theme" to switch.
