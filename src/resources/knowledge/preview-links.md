---
title: How long a preview link lasts
keywords: preview link, preview address, preview expires, preview ended, how long does the preview last, preview token, this preview has ended
link: /about.html
---
The address of a preview is valid for 2 hours by default. A site admin can change that in Settings, in the Preview card: any time from 10 minutes to a day. The Preview pane gets a new address by itself shortly before the old one ends, so an open pane carries on.

A link that is opened in a tab of its own, or copied, stops at its end and then says "This preview has ended. Open it again from the editor." A change of the time applies to links made after it. Switching the preview off stops every link at once.
