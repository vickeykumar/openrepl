On a phone the assistant is the third tab of the switch above the workspace, beside Editor and Terminal. It fills the screen there, so nothing floats over your code, and a dot on the tab shows when it is working or needs you. Leaving the tab does not stop it. The button next to Run makes the workspace use the whole screen, and the phone's back button brings the page back. Fork, Share, Apps and the commands are behind the three dots.
