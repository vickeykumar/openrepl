Press Share and send the link. Anyone who opens it sees your terminal and editor live and can type along, and there is a chat for talking while you work. A joined guest is announced in your session. Use this to pair on a problem, help a friend, or teach. The link follows the session, so it shows what you are doing now, not a snapshot. Opening Share starts a timed link: a guest chooses 30 minutes or 1 hour, a signed-in user up to 7 days. The link stays the same through reloads until it ends, with a countdown, Add time and End session. When it ends or runs out, everyone on it is told and all its data is deleted. If you close your editor, the people on your link are told you have left, can read the code and copy it to their own editor, and are connected again when you return.
