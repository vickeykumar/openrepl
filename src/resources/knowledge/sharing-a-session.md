Press Share and send the link. Anyone who opens it sees your terminal and editor live and can type along, and there is a chat for talking while you work. A joined guest is announced in your session. Use this to pair on a problem, help a friend, or teach. The link follows the session, so it shows what you are doing now, not a snapshot.
